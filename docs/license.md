## FAQ

??? question "Do I need a commercial license?"

    **If you're comfortable sharing all your code** (infrastructure, deployment scripts, modifications), you can use AGPL-3.0.

    **If you want to keep any part private**, you need a commercial license.

    **If you're running Caution as a service for others**, you likely need a commercial license.

??? question "Can I deploy proprietary applications on Caution?"

    Yes. The applications you deploy *on* Caution remain yours with their own licenses. This license only covers Caution itself, not what you run on it.

??? question "I'm a non-profit or educational institution"

    Contact us. We offer special pricing for non-profits, educational institutions, and open source projects. Many qualify for free commercial licenses.
